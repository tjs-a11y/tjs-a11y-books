What the publisher description covers

The publisher presents this title as an accessible introduction to the cryptocurrency market, with coverage of major digital assets and the blockchain technology behind them. Its description identifies these subject areas:

  • Bitcoin, Ethereum and Dogecoin as examples of cryptocurrencies.
  • Blockchain technology and the wider digital-currency landscape.
  • Foundational and more advanced cryptocurrency trading and investment concepts.
  • Risk management and themes shaping the future of the digital economy.

This summary follows the publisher description; it does not imply that any strategy produces a particular return.

How it differs from Mastering Cryptocurrency Trading

Mastering the Crypto Market is described as a broad introduction to cryptocurrencies, blockchain and market participation. Mastering Cryptocurrency Trading has a more specific trading-mechanics focus, including market, limit and stop orders, liquidity, moving averages, RSI, MACD and fundamental analysis.
