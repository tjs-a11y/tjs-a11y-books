The issue to understand

People researching decentralized organizations often struggle to assess treasury concentration, spending proposals, token reserves, governance controls, and long-term financial sustainability.

Key perspective

A DAO treasury can reveal how an organization funds development, incentives, operations, and future growth. Examine treasury composition, spending authority, governance safeguards, and transparency rather than assuming a decentralized structure automatically guarantees sound financial management.

Use these ideas as a starting point for independent research. Evaluate the details of any asset, platform, strategy, or organization rather than treating a general framework as a prediction or recommendation.
