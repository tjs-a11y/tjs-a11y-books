# Reader review request — manuscript back matter

Use the matching paragraph on the final page of each print and ebook manuscript. Keep it separate from any purchase incentive or free bonus. Do not ask for a positive review; ask for an honest one.

## Mastering Cryptocurrency Trading

**Enjoyed the book?** If this book helped you better understand cryptocurrency trading concepts, please consider leaving an honest review on Amazon or Goodreads. Your candid feedback—positive, mixed or critical—helps other readers decide whether this book is right for them. Thank you for reading.

## Editorial and platform safeguards

- Never condition a free chapter, bonus, refund, discount or other benefit on leaving a review.
- Never ask specifically for a positive or five-star review.
- Never write, purchase, or arrange reviews on a reader's behalf.
- Confirm the correct Amazon/Goodreads book pages before adding linked buttons to the manuscript.
