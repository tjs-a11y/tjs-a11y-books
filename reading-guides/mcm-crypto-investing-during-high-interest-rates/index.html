Cryptocurrency Investing During High Interest Rates

Higher borrowing costs and changing risk appetite can affect speculative asset valuations. Consider real yields, liquidity, funding conditions, and portfolio resilience when assessing crypto exposure.

A practical framework

Higher borrowing costs and changing risk appetite can affect speculative asset valuations.

  1. Track relevant policy rates, real yields, dollar conditions, and broader liquidity measures with their release dates.
  2. Distinguish a change in the data from a change in market expectations about that data.
  3. Compare macro context with crypto-specific liquidity, leverage, and market structure.
  4. Build scenarios for tighter, neutral, and easier financial conditions rather than assuming one outcome.
  5. Review whether exposure still fits the investor's time horizon and loss capacity.

Key idea to carry forward

Consider real yields, liquidity, funding conditions, and portfolio resilience when assessing crypto exposure.

Use this as a starting framework, then test assumptions against the underlying information and your own circumstances. A checklist can improve consistency; it cannot remove uncertainty.

Questions to review before acting

  • What is known, and what is still an assumption?
  • Which costs, constraints, or risks are easy to overlook?
  • What evidence would change the current plan?
  • What records should be kept so the decision can be reviewed later?
