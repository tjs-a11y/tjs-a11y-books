Mediterranean Bean and Lentil Soup Recipes

Finding filling make-ahead meals can be challenging during colder months or busy weeks. Bean and lentil soups offer adaptable, fiber-rich meal options using vegetables, broth, herbs, and pantry staples.

A practical framework

Finding filling make-ahead meals can be challenging during colder months or busy weeks.

  1. Choose beans, lentils, vegetables, or whole grains as the foundation and decide whether the dish is a main or side.
  2. Build flavor with aromatics, herbs, citrus, spices, and a measured amount of olive oil.
  3. Adjust texture with broth, tomatoes, grains, or vegetables, adding ingredients in an order that matches cooking time.
  4. Taste and adjust seasoning near the end, then portion for the number of people being served.
  5. Store leftovers promptly and reheat thoroughly using appropriate food-safety practices.
