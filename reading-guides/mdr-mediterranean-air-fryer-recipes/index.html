Mediterranean-Inspired Air Fryer Recipes

Air fryer users often need fresh ideas beyond heavily breaded or packaged foods. Prepare vegetables, fish, and other ingredients with olive oil, herbs, and simple seasoning for convenient meals.

A practical framework

Air fryer users often need fresh ideas beyond heavily breaded or packaged foods.

  1. Choose the protein and portion size, then select vegetables, beans, or whole grains that complete the meal.
  2. Season with a flexible combination of olive oil, citrus, garlic, and herbs; adjust to the ingredients and preferences on hand.
  3. Use a cooking method suited to the ingredient and thickness. Follow a reliable food-safety temperature guide for meat and seafood.
  4. Prepare a simple side while the main ingredient cooks so the meal finishes together.
  5. Record the seasoning and timing that worked, and adapt the next batch rather than assuming every appliance or cut cooks identically.

Key idea to carry forward

Prepare vegetables, fish, and other ingredients with olive oil, herbs, and simple seasoning for convenient meals.
