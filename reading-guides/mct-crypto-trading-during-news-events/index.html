Trading Cryptocurrency Around Major News Events

News can create fast price gaps, spread widening, and execution uncertainty. Prepare for event risk by reducing exposure where appropriate, defining invalidation, and avoiding assumptions about direction.

A practical framework

News can create fast price gaps, spread widening, and execution uncertainty.

  1. Define the market condition and the setup you intend to trade; avoid mixing several strategies into one rule set.
  2. Write the entry trigger, invalidation point, position-size method, and exit plan before committing capital.
  3. Check liquidity, volatility, fees, scheduled events, and platform-specific execution details.
  4. Keep a journal of the setup, execution, outcome, and any deviation from the plan.
  5. Review results over a meaningful sample and change rules only when evidence supports the change.

Key idea to carry forward

Prepare for event risk by reducing exposure where appropriate, defining invalidation, and avoiding assumptions about direction.

Use this as a starting framework, then test assumptions against the underlying information and your own circumstances. A checklist can improve consistency; it cannot remove uncertainty.

Questions to review before acting

  • What is known, and what is still an assumption?
  • Which costs, constraints, or risks are easy to overlook?
  • What evidence would change the current plan?
  • What records should be kept so the decision can be reviewed later?
