Families and caregivers

Family-Friendly Mediterranean Diet Meals

Cooking one meal for different preferences can make healthy family routines harder to maintain. Use build-your-own bowls, sheet-pan meals, pasta, and familiar ingredients so family members can customize their plates.

A practical framework

Cooking one meal for different preferences can make healthy family routines harder to maintain.

  1. Choose a meal format that fits the available time, equipment, and number of servings.
  2. Build the dish from familiar ingredients and substitute based on availability, season, and preference.
  3. Prepare ingredients before cooking and note which items need longer cooking times.
  4. Taste, adjust seasoning, and record the actual yield so portions are predictable.
  5. Store leftovers safely and reuse ingredients in a different meal to reduce waste.
