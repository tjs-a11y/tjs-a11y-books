What the book covers

The book's listed scope moves from how cryptocurrency markets work into the concepts readers encounter when studying trades and price behavior. It covers:

  • Order types: market, limit, and stop orders, and how order choices affect execution.
  • Liquidity and price setting: why the ease of buying or selling and the available market affect prices.
  • Technical analysis: chart-based concepts including moving averages, the Relative Strength Index (RSI), and MACD.
  • Fundamental analysis: broader economic factors that may influence cryptocurrency prices.

This overview summarizes the retailer-listed subject matter; it does not promise a trading result or imply that any indicator predicts future prices.

A practical way to use a trading guide

Learn the vocabulary first

Make sure you can explain an order type and its trade-offs before using it on a platform.

Understand indicator limits

Moving averages, RSI, and MACD describe selected market data; they are not guarantees or standalone signals.

Keep risk visible

Crypto assets can be volatile. Review fees, liquidity, platform terms, and risk disclosures before making decisions.

Who may find this overview useful

Readers who want to become more familiar with crypto-trading terminology and common analysis concepts before researching a market may find the topic coverage relevant. It is educational material, not individualized investment advice, and it cannot remove the risk of loss.
